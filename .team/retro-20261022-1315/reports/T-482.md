- Two observed hint weaknesses: travel distances stay fixed at the gathering destination, and returning from a completed tower trip temporarily produces an eastward instruction. I inferred the correct return direction from the map and prior tower instruction rather than following the conflicting hint.
- Screenshots show a readable map/inventory/control arrangement. After character creation, the next-step panel is below the market on the full-page image. The controller exposes the whole page text, so this run cannot establish whether a normal viewport user would notice that panel promptly.
- The market explains total price and lack of inventory reservation. Listing, purchase, sold status, insufficient-stock response and cancellation all had visible feedback. A stale listing still presents an enabled Buy button; stock insufficiency is revealed on purchase.
- No unavailable-control errors or unexpected operational errors occurred. The single rejected purchase was intentional exploration of the stated no-reservation behavior. There was no request for help and no prolonged idle/stuck interval; intervals include reading responses/screenshots and reporting observations. The empty market was the only supply-dependent moment.
