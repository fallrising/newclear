## Risks and Follow-ups

The barrier does not revoke an already-admitted caller's ability to invoke a lower-level mutation helper. Future reservation callers must enter only after writers are quiesced and in-flight work is zero. This slice must not claim production fresh execution, accepted generation commit, barrier release, or cross-controller fencing.

Scope limitation (material; independently characterized): `labops.ClusterLock.check_private_root` detects replacement during an active lock, but a restarted controller deliberately pointed at another backing directory cannot see the old reservation. `test_repointed_root_is_outside_same_backing_root_contract` confirms new admission while the original pending path still exists under the old root. Stable trusted backing-root identity and external takeover fencing are prerequisites; this is not protection against arbitrary local owner path replacement. `pending_generation.reserve` has no release/bypass and `assert_no_pending` denies on any pending path, regardless of completion claims or invalid record contents.
