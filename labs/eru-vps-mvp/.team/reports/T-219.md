## Risks and Follow-ups

This is preparation and local observation, not a production executor, remote baseline verifier, accepted-run writer or barrier release. Local conservative freshness policy allows at most one hour for owner authorization and fifteen minutes for observations. All host baselines must match reviewed machine IDs and contain four distinct machine, boot and host-key identities; full etcd/runtime/residue baseline capture remains future work. External material files verify locally readable evidence bytes, not physical off-host location or credentials usability.

Inspection is observational and cannot admit mutations. Missing private root returns blocked conservatively. Existing inputs must already have owner-private permissions; the loader does not repair them. The source-state/time override is an explicit fixture seam, not exposed through the production CLI. Same-UID malicious writers are outside the cooperative controller/filesystem trust boundary. The failure marker is best-effort if storage itself stops accepting writes; a prepared observation is never proof of a previous fsync outcome or authorization to execute. Complete live stages, generation CAS/seal and future resume remain separate work.
