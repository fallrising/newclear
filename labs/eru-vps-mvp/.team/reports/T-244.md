## Summary

Implemented the fixed directory-only host helper with a permanent external journal, strict data-only action validation, descriptor-relative safe IO and durable intent before target creation. Both new claim and target directories are compared against their first recorded inode, opened descriptor and empty contents. Canonical journal bytes, safe modes, host incarnation and original descriptors remain checked through completion and readonly recovery. Existing targets are never adopted, repaired or overwritten; failed operations remain non-replayable. A losing claimant cannot write a failure marker into a winner's journal.

Its default preserves staging behavior; the directory helper opts out of opening the initially absent target. The new external journal receives explicit private-mode checks independently of the old helper's path-specific rules.
