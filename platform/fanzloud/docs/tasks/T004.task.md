# Outcome

Compose the accepted credential boundary and pinned Cloud contract into the private provider-
specific P0 task orchestrator without mixing E2 submission, E2 lifecycle, or E0 diff retrieval in
one implementation task.

# Decomposition

| Child | Contract Unit | Atomicity | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| T004A | CU-CLOUD-P0-01 | E2 | Trusted fixed-command runner, durable submit outcome, and bounded list reconciliation |
| T004A1 | CU-CLOUD-P0-01 | E2 | Prompt-free observation and explicit unknown terminalization required by T004B |
| T004B | CU-AGT-P0-02 | E2 | Provider task lifecycle, explicit cancellation projection, and recovery policy |
| T004C | CU-CLOUD-P0-02 | E0 | Bounded provider-managed task diff retrieval |

T004A, its T004A1 recovery amendment, T004B, and T004C are independently Accepted. ADR-0003 keeps
CU-BKD-01 in T180 rather than freezing a second P0 backend port. The combined gates and fresh
composition review pass.

# Inputs

- TD §§1.6–1.7, 2.2–2.3, 8.2–8.10, 9.2–9.6, 11.1–11.4, 14, and 15.0
- ADR-0002 and ADR-0003
- Accepted T002 and T003

# Deliverables

- T004A plus its T004A1 amendment, T004B, and T004C independently accepted with normative
  specifications.
- Full launcher-level P14 and exact P15 evidence in T004A.
- Exact local-only cancellation semantics and an E0 diagnostic-write prevention gate.
- Combined workspace, security, fault, no-local-execution, and fresh composition review evidence.

# Non-scope

HTTP, WebSocket, browser UI, local repository checkout or tools, `cloud apply`, arbitrary
environment/repository selection, multi-user tenancy, and a live provider smoke.

# Invariants

- T004 never launches repository-controlled code or copies local credentials to a provider task.
- An interrupted submit without a durably recorded task ID is `OutcomeUnknown` and is never retried
  automatically.
- Provider output, prompts, diffs, and credential material remain bounded and redacted at their
  public and durable boundaries.
- A child blocked by a `[TD-GAP]` cannot become Ready by weakening its atomicity or non-guarantees.
