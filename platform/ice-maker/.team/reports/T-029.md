## Summary

Implemented and independently hardened the bounded raster OCR adapter. It now
provides one all-or-nothing source-bound decode/tile/OCR/TSV/evidence path,
private raster handling, tracked image/pixel/tile limits, strict public value
validation, deterministic sequential execution below the two-worker ceiling,
UTF-8 byte bounds, Tesseract version evidence, and process-group cleanup.
Pillow is pinned to `12.3.0` at the adapter boundary.

## Risks and Follow-ups

Pillow and Tesseract are unavailable on the host, so real codec/OCR quality is
not claimed. Decoder and Tesseract seams cover the unavailable boundary, while
an actual local subprocess proves output RLIMIT, timeout, group cleanup, and
scratch removal. Container/runtime and representative-corpus evidence remain
downstream gates.
