## REWORK 1 — orchestrator evidence

The six focused tests pass, but two public paths weaken the stated provenance
and replay contracts:

- `Artifact.grounded_citations` accepts arbitrary identifiers such as
  `chunk-a`; these must be exact lowercase SHA-256 Phase 4 chunk identities.
- The module-level `promote()` creates a fresh ledger on every call, so the
  same critic and human records can be replayed indefinitely. A caller can
  bypass the replay-protected `PromotionStateMachine` through this public API.

Required rework:

- [ ] Require exact 64-hex citation identities and update all tests to use
  realistic digests; add uppercase/short/non-string failure cases.
- [ ] Remove the stateless replay bypass or require a caller-owned retained
  `PromotionStateMachine` on every public promotion path. Prove identical
  decisions fail on the second call through every exposed path.
- [ ] Add terminal-principle, parent-hypothesis, decision-type, actor, digest,
  and failure-atomicity regressions. Failed decisions must not consume a later
  valid decision.
- [ ] Refactor long lines, rerun focused/full tests, and replace the report with
  exact evidence.
