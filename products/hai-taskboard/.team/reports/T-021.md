## Summary

Independent T-021 acceptance decision: **FAIL**.

All pinned Go quality checks passed and the domain/reconciliation source and tests were inspected in
full. `WorkItem.Transition` itself rejects `PhaseDone` regardless of every public guard boolean.
However, the candidate exposes a separate public authority bypass: `NewWorkItem` accepts
`PhaseDone` and returns a Done WorkItem without a `CompletionSubject`, Done-gate evaluation, or
`CompletionRecord`. This violates ADR-002's “Only `CompleteWorkItem` may enter Done” decision and
HAI-STATE-004, and means T-020 cannot be accepted.

The failing candidate boundary is
`products/hai-taskboard/backend/internal/domain/workitem.go:80-84` (SHA-256
`adb6de4a76b1a6ce4364e56906de2089dcb6f765d078605ae7e24a3b4c94797c`). Its public `phase`
parameter accepts every `Phase.Valid()` value, including `PhaseDone`.
