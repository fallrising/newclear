Motion Prompt #016 · motion/parallax

Meaning: Near and far layers move at different speeds to show depth
Example request (adapt to your project): Split this landscape into three layers and scroll them with parallax, nearer layers faster.
What this demo shows: A landscape pans sideways; near layers move more and far layers move less, creating depth.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- Camera pan: ±108 px on a sine wave, one round trip per 4.7 s.
- Each layer is offset by pan × factor in the opposite direction. Factors from back to front: stars 0.05, moon 0.1, far ridge 0.28, middle ridge 0.6, front hill and trees 1.25, nearest dark shapes 2.1.
- Far layers use darker colours close to the background, nearer layers brighter ones; the nearest layer is a large dark silhouette along the bottom.
- Every layer is drawn at least 200 px beyond both sides of the frame so its ends never show.
When to use it: Use it to give flat artwork or photos depth: backgrounds, landscapes, scrolling scenes.
