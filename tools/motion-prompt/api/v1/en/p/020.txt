Motion Prompt #020 · motion/zoom-through

Meaning: Zooms into an object and passes through it into the next scene
Example request (adapt to your project): Zoom through the hole of the "O" in the logo into the next scene, 0.6 s, accelerating.
What this demo shows: The camera pushes into the hole of a letter O and comes out in the next scene.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- Scene X (ZOOM on yellow) → scene Y (GO! on blue) → scene X, 2.35 s each.
- The current scene is scaled around the centre of an O's hole (radius 24.5). Maximum scale is 1.08× the value at which the hole covers the whole frame (about 14×).
- Scale = maxScale^p (exponential zoom, which reads as constant speed).
- p = 0.8 × cubic ease-in (0.25–1.2 s) + 0.2 × cubic ease-out (1.2–1.65 s): accelerate in, slow down at the end.
- Inside the hole the next scene is placed at 1/maxScale. When the zoom ends it is exactly at full size, and the view switches to the next scene.
When to use it: Use it to dive into a logo or letter and move to the next scene with momentum.
