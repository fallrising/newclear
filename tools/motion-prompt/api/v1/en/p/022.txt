Motion Prompt #022 · motion/light-sweep

Meaning: A band of light crosses a surface
Example request (adapt to your project): After the logo settles, add one light sweep from top-left to bottom-right, subtle.
What this demo shows: A band of light crosses the surface of a logo badge at an angle.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- One sweep per 2.35 s. The light is drawn only inside a clip of the badge (400 × 112, radius 28, blue gradient).
- Light band: a 116 px wide white gradient (opacity 0.72 in the middle, 0 at the edges) plus a thin 12 px band (opacity 0.5) 78 px away, rotated 0.42 rad (about 24°).
- It moves from 130 px left of the badge to 130 px right of it (660 px) at 0.45–1.5 s, quadratic ease-in-out.
- Sparkle: a four-point star at the top-right corner at 1.38–1.98 s; size 30 × sin(πq) × (1 + 0.2(1 − q)), rotating 0.9 rad.
- The badge itself does not move.
When to use it: Use it to give a logo, badge or button a premium finish.
