Motion Prompt #051 · ui/bottom-sheet

Meaning: A panel slides up from the bottom of the screen
Example request (adapt to your project): Show the details in a bottom sheet when "View details" is tapped: 40% of the screen tall, rising over 0.4 s, closing when dragged down.
What this demo shows: A finger taps "View details" and a panel rises from the bottom; dragging its handle down closes it.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. A finger (semi-transparent circle, radius 17, white rim) taps the "View details" pill (170×36, bottom centre) at 1.0 s.
- Open amount: 0→1 over 1.05–1.45 s with easeOutCubic. Full-card scrim rgba(18,20,28) at opacity 0.36·a.
- Sheet: white, 136 tall, top corners radius 18, top edge at y = 191 − 136·a. Shadow blur 22, offset y −4. A 52×6 grab handle at top centre.
- Inside: a bold title bar and two rows with 26×26 icons.
- Close: the finger lands on the handle (271, 68) at 2.75 s and drags down over 3.0–3.6 s; a is multiplied by (1 − easeInOutCubic over 3.0–3.6 s), so the sheet follows the finger off the bottom.
When to use it: Use it on mobile to show details or choices while keeping the current screen in view.
