Motion Prompt #052 · ui/accordion

Meaning: Click a heading to show or hide its content
Example request (adapt to your project): Make the FAQ an accordion: the clicked item opens over 0.35 s and its arrow turns 180°.
What this demo shows: Clicking the first of three headings opens its content and pushes the rows below down; clicking again closes it.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. Three headings (494 wide, 34 tall, radius 8, gap 6) with a down chevron at the right.
- The cursor reaches the first heading at 0.95 s and clicks at 1.0 s and 3.3 s.
- Open amount ex: 0→1 over 1.0–1.35 s with easeInOutCubic, then 1→0 over 3.3–3.62 s.
- The first row grows from 34 to 92 tall (+58·ex) and pushes the rows below down; its two content lines are clipped to the row and fade in with opacity ex.
- While open, the chevron rotates 180°·ex, the border turns light blue and the heading blue.
When to use it: Use it for FAQs or long details where people scan the headings and open only what they need.
