Motion Prompt #067 · ui/mega-menu

Meaning: A wide panel of grouped links that opens from the main menu
Example request (adapt to your project): Give "Products" in the header a mega menu: on hover, drop a full-width panel in 0.25 s with three columns of links and an image.
What this demo shows: Hovering over "Products" in the header drops a full-width panel with three columns of links and an image; it closes when the cursor leaves.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. A 42-tall header with "Products, Pricing, Support" (in the selected language, 16 px, each with a down chevron).
- The cursor only hovers: it reaches "Products" at 1.0 s. Open amount a: 0→1 over 1.05–1.3 s with easeOutCubic; "Products" turns blue with an underline.
- Panel: white, full width, 146 tall, dropping 12 px into place under the header while fading in with a. Shadow blur 18, offset y 8.
- Inside: three columns 130 px apart (a blue heading and three links each) and a 104×112 picture at the right. The cursor moves down and the second link of the second column is highlighted light blue from 1.75 to 2.9 s.
- Close: the cursor leaves the panel from 2.9 s; it fades over 3.15–3.4 s (1 − easeInOutCubic).
When to use it: Use it on sites with many products or sections so visitors can see the whole range at once.
