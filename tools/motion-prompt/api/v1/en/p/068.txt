Motion Prompt #068 · ui/step-indicator

Meaning: Shows which step of a process you are on
Example request (adapt to your project): Add a step indicator above the sign-up form with four steps: Details, Review, Payment, Done; completed steps show a check.
What this demo shows: Each click on "Next" turns the next of four step circles into a check and extends the blue line to the following step.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. Four steps ("Details, Review, Payment, Done" in the selected language) at x 80, 207, 334, 461, y 35; circles of radius 17 joined by a 4 px grey line.
- The cursor clicks "Next" (110×38, bottom right) at 0.9, 1.7 and 2.5 s.
- On each click the current step becomes a blue circle with a white check and the next becomes the current one (blue circle, white number). Steps not reached are white with a grey rim.
- The blue line grows 127 px per click over 0.3 s with easeInOutCubic; after the third click all four are checked.
- Reset: the indicator fades out over 3.75–4.05 s and fades back in at step 1 over 4.05–4.3 s.
When to use it: Use it in multi-step flows such as sign-up or checkout to show progress and what is left.
