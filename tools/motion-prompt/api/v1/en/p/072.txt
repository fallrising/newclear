Motion Prompt #072 · ui/empty-state

Meaning: The screen shown when there is nothing to list yet
Example request (adapt to your project): When the list is empty, show an empty state: a line icon, "Nothing here yet" and an add button, fading in over 0.35 s.
What this demo shows: Deleting the two list rows with their trash icons brings up an empty-box drawing, "Nothing here yet" and an "Add" button.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. A two-row list (494×48, radius 8, a trash icon at the right).
- The cursor clicks the trash icons at 0.95 s and 1.65 s. Each row slides 60 px left while fading out over 0.3 s (easeInOutCubic); the second row moves up 58 px into the first slot.
- Over 1.95–2.3 s (linear) the empty state fades in while rising 8 px: a blue line drawing of an inbox tray, "Nothing here yet" (16 px, in the selected language) and a blue "Add" button (112×34).
- The cursor clicks "Add" at 3.3 s; the empty state fades over 3.25–3.45 s and the two rows come back from the left over 3.35–3.7 s with easeOutCubic.
When to use it: Use it for first-use screens or no-results pages to tell people what to do next.
