Motion Prompt #073 · lp/parallax-scrolling

Meaning: On scroll, background layers move slower than the content
Example request (adapt to your project): Make the hero background a parallax scroll: move it at 0.3× the scroll distance.
What this demo shows: As the page scrolls down, the headline moves up normally while the sky and sun, far hills and near hills move more slowly.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- A browser window with a 511 × 168 viewport. Scroll: 0→120 px at 0.3–1.9 s, 120→0 at 2.9–4.3 s (cubic ease-in-out).
- Each layer moves by scroll × factor: sky and sun 0.15, far hills 0.4, near hills 0.7.
- Headline (28 px), button and the next section move at 1.0 (normal scrolling).
- The next section (white) starts at the bottom of the hero (168 px) and slides over the slower background.
- The scrollbar thumb on the right moves with the scroll.
When to use it: Use it to give the hero or a section photo a sense of depth.
