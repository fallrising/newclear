Motion Prompt #075 · lp/sticky-header

Meaning: The header stays at the top while the page scrolls
Example request (adapt to your project): Make the header a sticky header, with a soft shadow once it sticks to the top.
What this demo shows: The announcement strip scrolls away, but the header below it stops at the top edge and the content scrolls underneath.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- Scroll: 0→110 px at 0.4–1.3 s, 110→250 px at 1.9–2.8 s, 250→0 at 3.4–4.4 s (cubic ease-in-out).
- At the very top, a 22 px announcement strip (navy); below it a 34 px header (logo, three menu items, button).
- Header position = max(22 − scroll, 0): once the strip is gone, it stops at the top edge (position: sticky; top: 0).
- Over the next 24 px of scroll, a 12 px shadow under the header fades from 0 to 0.16 opacity.
- The content (photo, heading, three cards) scrolls normally under the header.
When to use it: Use it to keep the menu and the call-to-action within reach on a long page.
