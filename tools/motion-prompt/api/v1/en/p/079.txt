Motion Prompt #079 · lp/scroll-progress-bar

Meaning: A bar at the top shows how far you have read
Example request (adapt to your project): Add a scroll progress bar at the top of the article page, 4 px thick, in the brand orange.
What this demo shows: As the article is read a bit at a time, the line along the top grows from the left and reaches the far end at the bottom of the page.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- The article scrolls 0→430 px in four 110 px steps, each moving for 0.45 s then pausing 0.4 s (cubic ease-in-out).
- It returns 430→0 at 3.75–4.55 s.
- A 6 px bar along the top of the viewport: light grey track, orange fill.
- Fill width = 511 px × scroll / 430, the scroll fraction itself (the same as CSS animation-timeline: scroll()).
When to use it: Use it on long articles to show how much is left to read.
