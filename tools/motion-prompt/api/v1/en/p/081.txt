Motion Prompt #081 · lp/hover-lift

Meaning: On hover, the element rises with a deeper shadow
Example request (adapt to your project): Give the cards a hover lift: up 8 px with a deeper shadow, over 0.3 s.
What this demo shows: Only the card under the cursor lifts slightly and its shadow spreads; it settles back when the cursor leaves.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- Three cards (146 × 128, radius 10). The cursor rests on each, left to right, for about 1 s.
- On hover the card rises 10 px over 0.3 s (cubic ease-out); on leave it returns over the same 0.3 s (like a CSS transition).
- Shadow, changed together with the lift: blur 6→22 px, y offset 2→12 px, opacity 0.08→0.22.
- Light grey page, white cards, so the shadow reads.
When to use it: Use it to make cards and buttons look clickable.
