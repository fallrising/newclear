Motion Prompt #098 · photo/rim-light

Meaning: A light from behind that outlines the subject's edge
Example request (adapt to your project): Portrait on a black background with rim lights from both rear sides; the face stays dark and only the outline of the head and shoulders glows.
What this demo shows: The surroundings go dark and two lights behind the person, left and right, draw thin bright edges only along both sides of the head and shoulders; the plan at the bottom right shows where the two lights are.
Demo specification (independent of the example request):
- Scene 1: a head-and-shoulders figure in front of a horizon at y 150 (head centre x 270, y 78, radius 29 px, no face or hair).
- Background: light scene → dark scene (sky #2B2D33, ground #24252A); figure #4D5A75 → #1F2128
- Bright edges: two copies of the figure in amber #FFC75A, shifted 3.5 px left and right and 2.5 px up, placed behind the dark figure (3.5 px wide edges)
- Plan at the bottom right (66×66 px): lights at ±135° seen from above (behind, left and right); top-right readout "±135°"
- Timing: the effect comes in from 0.4 s to 1.4 s, holds until 4.0 s and is gone again by 4.8 s (one pass = 5 s, cubic ease-in-out (about cubic-bezier(0.65,0,0.35,1)))
When to use it: To separate a person or product from a dark background and show its shape.
